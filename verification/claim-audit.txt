ABSTRACT AND THEOREM A
1. Finite, II_1, II_infinity, II and semifinite perfect-host languages are
   Sigma^0_2-complete: NEW. Upper bound: finite-corner overlap and recursive
   moment compactness. Lower bound: critical selector, effective embedding,
   normal commuting Boolean compiler and finite-algebra YES models.
2. Each fixed III_lambda language equals commuting perfection: NEW.
   Input mechanisms: Takesaki expectation theorem, Connes Section 3.6,
   Haagerup--Musat Lemma 3.8, Connes--Takesaki flow of weights.
3. Its Pi^0_1-completeness: NEW as the subtype conclusion; commuting Pi^0_1
   completeness is KNOWN (Slofstra; Mousavi--Nezhadi--Yuen Theorem 6.4).
4. III-ONLY Pi^0_2-completeness, with every hardness instance commuting
   perfect and quantum value one: NEW. All-instance KMS model and finite-tree
   approximate construction are given separately in Theorem C.
5. Type I and II_1 are incomparable: NEW as the combined conclusion.
   I not contained in II_1 uses only the new fixed one-bridge shift separator.
   II_1 not contained in I is KNOWN (ReadType Proposition 7.4 and Slofstra's
   nonclosure mechanism); the spatial-to-finite implication is credited to
   Cleve--Mittal, Cleve--Liu--Slofstra Theorem 5 and Slofstra.
6. Both languages are strict subsets of II_infinity: NEW combined conclusion,
   using standard finite amplification and the two credited separators.
7. I-ONLY and INF-ENTROPY-ONLY D_2(Sigma^0_2) classifications: NEW.
   All four incomparable entries use (Gs AND R*) OR (Gt AND T*).
   Gs finite-host membership is not assumed in these reductions.
8. II-ONLY and finite-entropy variant: NEW upper bounds and both hardness
   bounds; completeness is explicitly OPEN. No shared-family equivalence is
   inferred from separate completeness.
